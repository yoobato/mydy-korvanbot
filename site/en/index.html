About your notifications

New and edited posts
Existing posts are not sent when monitoring begins. We then notify you of new posts and send a separate “📝 게시글 수정” alert when a title or body changes. Edit alerts show the original modification date, or a clearly labelled detection date in Vancouver time if no modification date is available. The first edit check saves the current content as a baseline.
If something fails
If we cannot collect a post or send an alert, we try again during the next monitoring cycle.
Check frequency
We wait 60 minutes after completing each check. Posts in the recent lists are checked for edits every cycle; saved posts outside those lists are checked once a day. Notifications may arrive after the original publication time. Connection failures or changes to the official website can delay alerts or cause missed posts.
Images and attachments
We do not read text inside images or attached PDF/document files. We cannot detect file content replaced at the same URL or changes in the separate attachments section. For image-only posts, we send a reminder to open the original. Please check the original for full details.
